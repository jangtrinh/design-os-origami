## Purpose
Records what Lang's published deer crease pattern verifiably shows, so our own deer plan can be compared against a real reference without borrowing unsupported conclusions.

## When to Use / When NOT
- **ALLOWED**: comparing scale, symmetry and approximate circle count of a deer design with this reference.
- **NOT ALLOWED**: claiming which circle is which body part; neither fetched source labels them.
- **NOT ALLOWED**: reproducing or embedding the pattern; it is Robert J. Lang's copyrighted artwork. Attribution and links here are reference practice, not a license, and this file does not place the artwork under the repository license.

## Core Knowledge Content
Evidence labels: FACT = in a fetched source; INFERENCE = our reading of the rendered image; NOT VERIFIED = not established.

1. FACT: Lang's artwork page (https://langorigami.com/artwork/white-tailed-deer-opus-550/, fetched; sha256 f31e2afe3c96c972adbc94b7166ab3a8ce7007e313a748b6b9d9ed9ed7b08503) describes the model as one of his first TreeMaker designs, a subject that was one of the primary motivations for developing the underlying theory, folded from one uncut square of itajime washi paper. <!-- ease:source https://langorigami.com/artwork/white-tailed-deer-opus-550/ -->
2. FACT: the crease-pattern file (the PDF named in this file's frontmatter) is a single-page drawing of a square with a shaded circle packing; in the rendered image no labels are visible. Hidden text was not ruled out. <!-- ease:source https://langorigami.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/white_tailed_deer_cp.pdf -->
3. INFERENCE (visual, from a 110 dpi render): the layout is visually mirror-symmetric left to right (exact symmetry not verified). Shaded circular regions include two large interior circles at mid height, one medium interior circle near the top centre, three along the bottom edge (two quarter-disk corners and one half-disk), and many smaller clipped portions along the sides and top; the approximate visual count depends on how clipped portions are counted. An exact count is NOT VERIFIED; the image suggests more than eight regions.
4. NOT VERIFIED: any correspondence between these circles and legs, body, head, antlers or tail; any claim about how many tips a realistic deer needs; and whether our own tree maps onto this layout.

## Failure Modes (Mandatory)
- Reading circle regions as anatomical tips: clipped boundary portions are not additional independent flaps by themselves.
- Copying the pattern: it is copyrighted artwork; use only as a scale and symmetry reference.
- Over-reading a visual count: the figure is approximate and was not machine-extracted.
- Treating this file as validation of our seeds: it validates nothing about our ratios or topology.
