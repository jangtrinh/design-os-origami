# Attribution & Provenance (`es-origami-cad`)

This skill synthesizes mathematical origami theorems, industrial packaging engineering standards, and computational geometry:

1. **Huzita-Justin-Hatori Axioms**:
   - Humiaki Huzita (1989), Jacques Justin (1986), Koshiro Hatori (2001).
   - Geometric operations defining planar single-crease construction.
2. **Kawasaki's Theorem & Maekawa's Theorem**:
   - Toshikazu Kawasaki (1989) on single-vertex flat-foldability.
   - Jun Maekawa (1980s) on mountain/valley parity $|M - V| = 2$.
3. **Packaging Engineering Standards**:
   - **ECMA Code**: European Carton Makers Association standards for folding boxboard (SBS/FBB).
   - **FEFCO Code**: European Federation of Corrugated Board Manufacturers standards for flute cartons.
4. **Kinematic Fold Simulation**:
   - Rodrigues' rotation formula around arbitrary hinge axes in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
   - Three.js (r128) WebGL rendering engine (MIT License).
